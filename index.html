About Our Mission

AquaSense is a pioneering Smart Water Management solution born from the strategic partnership between AquaFlow Solutions and EcomEase.

We integrate high-precision IoT monitoring with no-code e-commerce automation to help households, MSMEs, and government bodies reduce water wastage by up to 50%. Our platform transforms water from a managed utility into a strategic resource through real-time tracking, AI-driven failure prediction, and automated billing.
